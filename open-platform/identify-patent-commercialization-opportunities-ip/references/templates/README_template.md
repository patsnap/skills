## Purpose

This report is a decision-support assessment of a defined technology scope. It combines reproducible patent metrics, a representative patent sample, technical analysis, and explicitly identified commercial evidence or evidence gaps. It is not an FTO opinion, valuation, market forecast, or investment recommendation.
