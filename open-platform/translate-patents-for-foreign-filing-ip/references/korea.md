# Korea Patent Translation Reference

## Scope

Use for a Korean patent-application filing-support draft based on Chinese source material.
Write in Korean patent style and verify current KIPO route, translation, form, sequence-listing,
and deadline requirements for the actual application.

## Source-faithfulness controls

- Preserve support from the Chinese priority/application text.
- Do not add technical effects, parameters, implementations, definitions, or fallback embodiments.
- Maintain a Chinese–Korean terminology register with source locators.
- Flag ambiguity, modifier attachment, omitted subjects, and destination-sensitive choices.
- Preserve claim category, dependency, numbering, ranges, units, formulas, and reference signs.

## Drafting focus

- Use Korean patent conventions rather than literal Chinese syntax.
- Keep 부, 모듈, 유닛, 장치, 수단, and 단계 consistent with the field and claim category.
- Make claim limitations, technical relationships, and executing subjects explicit.
- Preserve support between claims and the detailed description.
- Express effects moderately and only to the extent disclosed.

## Recommended section order

```text
발명의 명칭
기술분야
배경기술
발명의 내용
도면의 간단한 설명
발명을 실시하기 위한 구체적인 내용
청구범위
요약서
도면
서열목록(해당하는 경우)
```

## Claim-language controls

- Use 포함하는 for open inclusion language when appropriate to the supported meaning.
- Preserve 하나 이상, 복수의, 임의의, and 및/또는 scope accurately.
- Do not impose strict step order unless the source or technical logic requires it.
- Keep apparatus, system, method, process, and storage-medium categories distinct.
- Translate connection or coupling according to the actual structural, electrical,
  communication, fluid, optical, or logical relationship.
- Preserve optional, preferred, exemplary, and alternative language as non-mandatory.
- Flag possible clarity, support, enablement, unity, and amendment-basis issues.

## Translation QA

- Check that Korean syntax does not attach modifiers to the wrong technical element.
- Reconcile claim numbering, dependencies, subjects, and categories.
- Verify that optional embodiments remain optional.
- Match reference signs and figure labels to Korean terminology.
- Preserve ranges, units, formulas, algorithms, materials, and test conditions.
- Map independent-claim limitations to the Chinese source and record unresolved ambiguity.

## Professional review gate

Qualified Korean patent counsel and a competent Korean patent translator should review the draft
for the specific route, support, amendment, formal, and filing issues before submission.
