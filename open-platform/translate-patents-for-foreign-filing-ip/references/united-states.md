# United States Patent Translation Reference

## Scope

Use for a US utility-application filing-support draft based on Chinese source material.
Use US patent English and verify current USPTO route, form, translation, sequence-listing,
inventor declaration, information-disclosure, and deadline requirements for the actual filing.

## Source-faithfulness controls

- Preserve the Chinese disclosure and distinguish translation from substantive redrafting.
- Do not add definitions, advantages, embodiments, parameters, or fallback claims absent from the source.
- Log ambiguous source language and every counsel-approved substantive adaptation.
- Maintain a bilingual claim-term register and source locator for each independent-claim limitation.
- Keep priority support and possible new-matter issues visible for counsel.

## Drafting focus

- Preserve broad but supported claim language.
- Avoid unnecessary admissions or categorical prior-art statements in the Background.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives, absolute effects, and mandatory language.
- Preserve definitions, alternatives, examples, and implementation detail already disclosed.
- Flag potential means-plus-function treatment when translating modules, units, devices, or
  “configured to” and “means for” language.

## Recommended section order

```text
Title of the Invention
Cross-Reference to Related Applications, if applicable
Technical Field
Background
Summary
Brief Description of the Drawings
Detailed Description
Claims
Abstract
Drawings
Sequence Listing, if applicable
```

## Claim-language controls

- Use “comprising” for open inclusion language unless closed scope is clearly intended.
- Use “consisting of” or “consisting essentially of” only with explicit strategic approval and support.
- Introduce a singular element with “a” or “an” and use “the” consistently thereafter.
- Preserve singular/plural logic in “at least one,” “one or more,” and plurality expressions.
- Do not add mandatory order to method steps unless the source or technical logic requires it.
- Translate connection and coupling terms according to the actual structural, electrical,
  communication, fluid, optical, or logical relationship.
- Keep apparatus, system, method, process, composition, and storage-medium categories distinct.
- Flag antecedent-basis, indefiniteness, written-description, enablement, and functional-claim risks.

## Translation QA

- Reconcile claim numbering, dependency, multiple dependency, and category.
- Identify accidental narrowing by “must,” “only,” “all,” “always,” or rigid sequence wording.
- Check that module/unit wording does not introduce avoidable functional-claim ambiguity.
- Review Background language for statements that could be treated as applicant admissions.
- Preserve ranges, units, formulas, algorithms, materials, and test conditions exactly.
- Verify drawing signs and terminology across claims, description, abstract, and figures.
- Record unresolved ambiguity instead of supplying an unsupported meaning.

## Professional review gate

US patent counsel must review claim construction, support, priority, new matter, formal requirements,
and filing strategy for the specific application. Do not characterize the draft as a legal opinion or certified translation.
