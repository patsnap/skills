# Japan Patent Translation Reference

## Scope

Use for a Japanese patent-application filing-support draft based on Chinese source material.
Write in Japanese patent style and verify current JPO route, translation, form, sequence-listing,
and deadline requirements for the actual application.

## Source-faithfulness controls

- Preserve direct support from the Chinese priority/application text.
- Do not add embodiments, effects, parameters, definitions, or fallback positions.
- Maintain a Chinese–Japanese terminology register with source locators.
- Flag ambiguity, modifier attachment, omitted subjects, and jurisdiction-sensitive adaptations.
- Preserve claim category, dependency, numbering, ranges, units, formulas, and reference signs.

## Drafting focus

- Use idiomatic Japanese patent drafting rather than Chinese-literal sentence structure.
- Keep the Japanese equivalents for component (*bu*), means (*shudan*), apparatus (*sochi*), process (*kotei*), and step (*suteppu*) consistent with the technology and claim category.
- Make claim sentences grammatically complete and modifier relationships explicit.
- Express technical effects concretely and no more strongly than the source supports.
- Keep optional and preferred embodiments explicitly non-mandatory.

## Recommended section order

```text
Title of the Invention (*Hatsumei no Meisho*)
Technical Field (*Gijutsu Bun'ya*)
Background Art (*Haikei Gijutsu*)
Summary of the Invention (*Hatsumei no Gaiyo*)
Brief Description of the Drawings (*Zumen no Kantanna Setsumei*)
Mode for Carrying Out the Invention (*Hatsumei o Jisshi Suru Tame no Keitai*)
Claims (*Tokkyo Seikyu no Han'i*)
Abstract (*Yoyakusho*)
Drawings (*Zumen*)
Sequence Listing, if applicable (*Hairetsu Ichiran*)
```

## Claim-language controls

- Use *sonaeru* for open-ended inclusion language when appropriate to the supported meaning.
- Establish clear antecedent relationships for components, data, and processing subjects.
- Translate Chinese purpose language as a functional relationship only where needed and supported.
- Prefer supported concrete structure or processing over ambiguous result-only language.
- Avoid clauses so long that modifier scope becomes uncertain.
- Preserve only expressly or technically required step order.
- Keep product, apparatus, system, method, use, and storage-medium categories distinct.
- Flag wording that may affect clarity, support, enablement, unity, or amendment basis.

## Translation QA

- Check whether Chinese parallel phrases become ambiguous in Japanese.
- Reconcile claim dependencies, numbering, subjects, and categories.
- Match reference numerals and drawing labels to Japanese terms.
- Verify that *konomashiku wa* (“preferably”) and other optional language remains optional.
- Preserve ranges, units, formulas, materials, algorithms, and test conditions.
- Map independent-claim limitations to the source and record unresolved ambiguity.

## Professional review gate

Qualified Japanese patent counsel and a competent Japanese patent translator should review the draft
for the specific route, support, amendment, formal, and filing issues before submission.
