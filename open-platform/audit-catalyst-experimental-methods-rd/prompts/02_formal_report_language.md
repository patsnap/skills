# Formal report language

Write for research scientists, principal investigators, project leads, and technical reviewers. Use precise, restrained, evidence-calibrated English.

Avoid product language, hype, ridicule, anthropomorphic AI claims, and unexplained internal enums. Do not use “red flag,” “AI audit,” “agent plan,” or “risk radar” as report labels.

Prefer: `priority issue`, `audit conclusion`, `dimension-level finding`, `material under review`, `the current design does not support this claim`, and `required addition`.

Display judgments as `Sufficient`, `Partially specified—additional detail required`, `Insufficient to support the requirement`, or `Not applicable`. Distinguish a missing statement from evidence that a control was not performed.
