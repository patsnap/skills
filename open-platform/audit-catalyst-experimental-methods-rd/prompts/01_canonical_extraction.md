# Canonical method-element extraction

Use this prompt only when the script cannot read the input structure directly. Extraction must remain faithful; do not complete missing information.

Extract:

- material type: preparation steps, complete preparation-and-evaluation plan, draft R&D concept, paper method, patent example, or insufficient material;
- catalyst system and sample identifiers;
- preparation steps: reagents, amounts, solvent, order of addition, temperature, duration, atmosphere, separation, washing, drying, calcination, and reduction;
- sample design: target, blank, baseline, control, and the variable changed;
- evaluation plan: reaction, reactor, temperature, pressure, flow or space velocity, catalyst amount, pretreatment, and analytical method;
- characterization plan: XRD, XPS, TEM, BET, ICP, TPR/TPD, operando or other relevant methods;
- performance or mechanistic claims;
- replication, uncertainty, mass balance, safety, and waste-handling controls when stated.

Record missing information as `not specified`. Preserve the source language in quoted evidence and provide an English working translation only when needed.
